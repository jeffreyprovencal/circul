Cost envelope

Africa's Talking SMS rate: ~GHS 0.02 per SMS. At pilot scale (100 active users × 4 sends/month): ~GHS 8/month. At scale-up (10,000 users): ~GHS 800/month. Modest, scales linearly, pays for itself in retention.

Privacy: messages contain only role-relevant aggregates and IDs (e.g., COL-0042), never full names of counterparties, in line with Circul's tier-based privacy model. Recipients can opt out via Reply STOP per Africa's Talking compliance requirements.

Status: on the post-pilot roadmap. Captures the feedback raised during the 2026-04-24 WORK partnership conversation. Ready to ship as a small cron + AT notify integration once the production rebuild begins.
